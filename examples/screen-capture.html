--- layout: example.html title: Screen Capture Control shortdesc: A custom control that captures the map as a PNG image. docs: > This example shows a custom control that captures the current map as an image. Click the camera button to render the map into a canvas and show a preview on top of the map. From the preview, the image can be downloaded as a PNG file with a time stamped file name, or discarded to return to the map. The layer attributions are drawn onto the captured image. tags: "custom, control, capture, export, png, screenshot, svg, button, canvas" ---
