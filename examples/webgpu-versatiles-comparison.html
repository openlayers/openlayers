--- layout: example.html title: VersaTiles Colorful with Canvas2D and WebGPU shortdesc: Compares the Canvas2D and WebGPU vector tile renderers using the same flat style. docs: | The [VersaTiles Colorful](https://tiles.versatiles.org/assets/styles/colorful/style.json) style is translated into flat style rules, as in the [VersaTiles Colorful (with WebGPU)](webgpu-versatiles.html) example. The rules are used on a `VectorTile` layer (Canvas2D, left) and on a `WebGPUVectorTile` layer (WebGPU, right). Both maps share one view, so they always show the same extent. WebGPU is required. Canvas2D style functions do not provide `['zoom']`, so the Canvas2D layer evaluates the rules with the view's zoom level. tags: "webgpu, vector, tiles, declutter, compare, versatiles, flat style" experimental: true ---
